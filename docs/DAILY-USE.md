# Using the CRM day to day

A short guide for Tim. The CRM works on your phone and your computer.

## The one rule

**Nothing ever goes out without you.** The system only writes drafts. You approve them, and you do the sending: from Gmail, LinkedIn, Facebook, or by mail.

## Your morning (5–10 minutes)

1. **Open Home.** You'll see:
   - **Awaiting approval:** drafts waiting for you
   - **Today's tasks:** leads whose "next action" is due today or overdue
   - **Pipeline:** how many leads are at each stage
2. **Work the Queue** (the ✓ tab). For each draft:
   - Read it. Fix anything right in the text box.
   - Tap **Approve**, or open **Reject…** and say why in a few words. Your reasons and edits teach the system your style.
   - After approving, **copy** it and send it yourself (email, LinkedIn, Facebook…).
   - Tap **Mark sent**. That logs it on the person's timeline and moves the lead to "Contacted".
3. **Clear today's tasks.** Tap **Done** when one is finished, or open the lead and set a new next action and date.

## Adding people

- **Home → + Add contact** (or **Leads → + New lead**).
- Two fields are required every time:
  - **Source:** where they came from ("Referral from Chris at Example CPA", "Frisco Chamber directory").
  - **How we got this contact:** a short compliance record ("Met at chamber lunch 11/12, gave me his card").
- If the person is already in the CRM, it tells you and links to the existing record instead of creating a duplicate.
- **Referral partners** (CPAs, bankers, attorneys, P&C agents, advisors): choose contact type **Referral partner**. The Partners page lists the people you haven't touched in the longest time first.

## Logging what happened

On any lead or contact page, use **Log activity** for calls, meetings, replies and notes. If someone replies, log it as **Reply** and move the lead's stage.

## Do not contact

- If someone says "unsubscribe" or asks not to be contacted, open them and tap **Mark do-not-contact**. Any drafts for them are removed right away and no new ones can be created.
- To block a whole company or email domain, go to **Settings → Do-not-contact list**.

## Settings worth filling in now

- **Business postal address.** The law (CAN-SPAM) requires one in commercial email. A PO box is fine. Until it's filled in, email drafts show **[ADDRESS NEEDED]**, so you can't miss it.
- **Email signature.**
- **Templates.** When you submit wording to Experior compliance, mark it **Submitted**, then **Approved** once it's cleared.

## Import and export

- **Import / export → Download blank template** gives you a spreadsheet layout. Fill it in, save as CSV, and import it.
- Export any list to CSV whenever you want a backup or a spreadsheet.

## On your phone

Open the site in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it opens like an app. The bottom bar has Home, Queue, Leads, Partners and Settings.
